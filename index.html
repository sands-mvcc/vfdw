CIT Frameworks Module 1: AI Fundamentals
Academic Curriculum Unit

Module 1: Foundations of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has rapidly transitioned from computational theory to the core driver of modern technological ecosystems. In this unit, we analyze the architectural divide between mathematical algorithms, machine learning models, and modern large-scale neural transformers.

01 The AI Taxonomy

Students often conflate Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Deep Learning. They are best understood as nested domains of capability:

Narrow AI

System architectures engineered and optimized to compute specific target objectives (e.g. chess engines, firewall heuristics).

Machine Learning

Algorithms that extract statistical insights directly from raw training datasets without explicit hardcoded instructions.

Deep Learning

Multi-layered artificial neural network nodes optimized to process features through weights, biases, and active feedback loops.

03 The Intersection of AI and Cybersecurity

As a core element of cybersecurity education, securing machine learning modules is paramount. This intersection features dual challenges:

Adversarial Data Ingestion & Poisoning

Adversaries systematically craft corrupted datasets during model optimization training cycles to establish reliable backdoors inside classification layers.

Autonomous Defensive Heuristics

Utilizing fine-tuned pattern monitoring systems to identify atypical outbound enterprise payload distributions and anomalies faster than standard manual threat hunters.
